    The one-step palette energy increment #

    Phase 9 unit 4 (design freeze in ARCHITECTURE.md): a partition that is not palette-regular has a refinement gaining ε⁵ of palette energy, with the existing graph part-count recurrence k ↦ k · 2^(2k).

    The proof resolves one bad palette color: a failure yields a bad color c (exists_bad_of_not_isBinaryPaletteRegular); the existing directed exists_refinement_energy_increment applied to HasBinaryPairPalette M c produces a refinement Q ≤ P gaining ε⁵ for that color. Every other palette color's energy is refinement-monotone, so none can lose energy, and the aggregate palette energy gains at least ε⁵. Witnesses are not atomized for every color simultaneously — the part-count bound is exactly the single-color graph bound.

    ∃ Q ≤ P, binaryPaletteEnergy M P + ε ^ 5 ≤ binaryPaletteEnergy M Q ∧ Q.parts.card ≤ P.parts.card * 2 ^ (2 * P.parts.card)
