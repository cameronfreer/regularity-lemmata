    Block energy and superadditivity #

    The mass-weighted local energy of an ordered block (A, B) is pairDensity R A B ² · |A| · |B|. Its key property is superadditivity under disjoint covers (an Engel-form/Cauchy–Schwarz consequence), specialized to refinement fibers in blockEnergy_le_sum_refined — the engine behind energy monotonicity of the partition energy (RegularityLemmata/Partition/Energy.lean).
