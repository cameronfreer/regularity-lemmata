    Finite probabilistic-method union bound #

    The deterministic endpoint of the equal-block sampling argument. Once each bad trace/block event is represented by a finite set of permutations, a sum of their cardinalities strictly below the permutation-space cardinality produces one permutation avoiding every event.

    source
    theorem RegularityLemmata.card_biUnion_le_sum_card {Omega : Type u_1} {I : Type u_2} [Fintype I] [DecidableEq Omega] (bad : I → Finset Omega) :
    (Finset.univ.biUnion bad).card ≤ ∑ i : I, (bad i).card

    The union of all bad events has cardinality at most the sum of their cardinalities.

    If the sum of bad-event cardinalities is smaller than the finite sample space, some outcome avoids every bad event. This is the exact finite probabilistic-method step used after the hypergeometric/permutation concentration estimate.
