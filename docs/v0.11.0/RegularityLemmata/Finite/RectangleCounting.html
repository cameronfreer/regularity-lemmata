    Rectangle counting for exceptional sets #

    Double-counting identities between the cardinality of an exceptional set E : Finset (X × Y) on a rectangle, its fiber counts, and the iterated averageOn of its indicator — the bookkeeping that prices exceptional-set trace functions α(x) = 𝔼_B 1_E(x, ·) through the slicing machinery. All identities are guard-free (an empty side makes both sides 0).

    source
    theorem RegularityLemmata.card_product_inter_eq_sum_right {X : Type u_1} {Y : Type u_2} [DecidableEq X] [DecidableEq Y] (E : Finset (X × Y)) (U : Finset X) (V : Finset Y) :
    (U ×ˢ V ∩ E).card = ∑ y ∈ V, {x ∈ U | (x, y) ∈ E}.card

    Fiber count over the right side: the rectangle trace of E counted by columns.
